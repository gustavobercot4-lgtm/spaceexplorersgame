# Space Explorers
Abandoned, but not forgotten.

Space Explorers is a browser-based educational game where players launch from Earth, explore the Moon, Mars and deep space, and find 24 real spacecraft, landers, rovers and instruments that NASA left behind, building their own museum.

Team: Pillars of Creation
NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 - "Abandoned but not Forgotten"

Play: https://gustavobercot4-lgtm.github.io/spaceexplorersgame/spaceexplorersgame.html

Data: NASA, JPL and NSSDCA. Images: JPL Photojournal and NASA Image and Video Library.
Independent educational game, not officially affiliated with NASA.
